## Context

Political and institutional teams in Uruguay are operationally weak at execution follow-through — meetings, commitments, and citizen requests are tracked manually across WhatsApp, docs, and spreadsheets, causing poor accountability and loss of institutional memory. Civic Ops transforms these into a clear, auditable execution workflow for legislative, municipal, and political teams.
